# Research Paper

Link: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/334469078_Analysis_of_Thermal_Comfort_of_a_naturally_ventilated_Recreational_Vehicle

Excerpt:

We have designed a Recreational vehicle suited to the varying climatic conditions of India, with minimal HVAC loads but without having to compromise the thermal comfort and well-being of the occupants. We show that by altering the orientation, fabric, and glazing ratio, it is possible to substantially reduce energy consumption in these vehicles using sophisticated energy modelling software. We illustrate the complex interplay between the carrier fluids and obstructing structures within it and explain patterns of ventilation in shear generated turbulence. It is expected that this first design study on an RV, anywhere within the sub-continent, will garner attention from manufacturers linked to the tourism industry. We show that the designed vehicle will approximately consume only 25% of the energy consumed by its counterpart hotel rooms.
