# Experience

> The four roles the timeline shows, with the only metrics that may be published for each.

Every figure below is cleared by [content-guardrails](content-guardrails.md). Nothing here may
be widened without going back to that page first.

## Wipro — Project Engineer, digital health assistant

**Bengaluru, India · August 2018 – August 2024 · six years**

Joined through Wipro's graduate programme into a conversational-AI centre of excellence,
building chatbot proof-of-concepts across several platforms. One of those demos converted an
RFP in March 2020 into a live project, and he led chatbot development on the five-person team
that delivered it: a digital health assistant for **a European insurance client**, on Azure
with a Java backend, JavaScript bot logic and a React Native front end, integrating external
symptom-check and doctor-chat services over REST.

Publishable highlights:

- Led chatbot development on a five-person team after helping convert the RFP that created it.
- Product rated **4.4 stars across 1,000+ users** in Europe, APAC and MENA *(self-reported)*.
- Evaluated and selected a replacement conversational-AI platform at **half the incumbent's
  cost** *(self-reported, ~$100K/yr → ~$50K/yr)*, then planned and executed the migration
  August 2022 – January 2023 with no service disruption.
- Took over team leadership mid-migration when two teammates left, onboarding replacements and
  transferring project knowledge while still delivering the cutover on schedule.
- Integrated an AI/ML module that cut service calls **30%** *(self-reported)*, and redesigned
  NLP-driven user workflows to reduce friction.
- "Trail Blazers" recognition, Q3 FY2023-24.

**Genericization is mandatory.** No client name, no platform or vendor names. The stack is
cleared. The three self-reported figures carry that marker wherever they appear.

## William & Mary Information Technology — IT data strategy and analytics intern

**Williamsburg, VA · May 2025 – August 2025**

A two-person team reporting to the university's chief data officer, asked whether internal
performance measures were even pointed at the things that move external rankings.

- Decomposed the U.S. News ranking methodology into **17 metrics across 6 categories**, tracing
  each to its underlying data source.
- Inventoried **19 internal KPIs across 5 owning committees** and attempted to correlate the two
  sets. Presented to the CDO and the data team.
- Produced a data governance document mapping ownership and reporting timelines, which fed into
  the university's Workday rollout and a governance layer in Microsoft Fabric.
- Benchmarked **19 peer institutional research websites** (5 Virginia, 14 national), found
  interactive factbooks to be the standard against a static incumbent, and recommended a
  redesign and information hierarchy.
- Built a Python network graph mapping Workday spend-category relationships.

This is the most direct applied evidence for data governance in the whole profile — real
governance documents and a live spend map, not a framework studied in a course.

## Fort Monroe Authority — MBA corporate field consultancy

**Fort Monroe, VA · September – November 2025 · seven weeks**

Capstone consulting engagement, one of five consultants, for a state-created authority that is
mandated to largely pay for itself while stewarding a National Historic Landmark. He was the
primary interface with the client's leadership team and owned four deliverables.

Timeline entry stays short and points at the case study:
[case-study-fort-monroe](case-study-fort-monroe.md). Seven weeks, four deliverables, one of
five consultants; the client rated the engagement highly in an independent post-project review
and commissioned a follow-on engagement.

The seven-week compression is worth stating plainly — four deliverables including a designed
and analysed survey inside seven weeks is a throughput signal, not just a scope statement.

## William & Mary — teaching assistant, economics

**Williamsburg, VA · August 2025 – May 2026**

Microeconomics, macroeconomics and managerial economics for the MBA programme. Facilitated
weekly review sessions across demand theory, elasticity, market structures, pricing strategy and
managerial decision-making, and supported faculty with curriculum delivery.

## Ordering

Reverse chronological by start date: teaching assistant, Fort Monroe, W&M IT, Wipro. The
overlapping MBA-period roles read as concurrent, which they were.
