## The division of authority

**Linear owns what is planned and what its status is** — priority, milestone, backlog order,
open or done. **This repo owns what happened and why** — the wiki, the decisions, the measured
results, the things that were tried and rejected. Neither restates the other: a Linear issue
links its doc rather than copying a gate into a second place where it can rot.

## A warning about `sources/`

`portfolio-website-plan.md` and `design-suggestions.md` are **factually wrong about Deepayan**.
They describe projects, awards, metrics and a technology stack that do not exist. They are kept
because the structural thinking in them is sound and is where several design decisions came
from, but they are **never a content source**. See
[wiki/research-synthesis.md](wiki/research-synthesis.md) for the full account of what was kept
and what was discarded.

`research-paper-details.md` and `additional-cert-links.md` are real and can be trusted.
